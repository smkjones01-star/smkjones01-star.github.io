A little context.

I’m Sydney. I write about whatever I find worth paying attention to: books, cities, work, culture, law, people, and the small observations that tend to turn into larger questions.

I’m currently based in Washington, DC. Law is part of my life and it will show up here sometimes, but this site is less a professional portfolio than a place to think in public.

The longer pieces live under Essays. Notes are looser: shorter observations, recommendations, fragments, and things I’m still working out.

Mostly, this is a home for writing that I want to keep.
